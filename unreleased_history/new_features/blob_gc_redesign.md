BlobDB garbage collection now uses per-file garbage ratio targeting instead of age-based batch selection. New options: `blob_file_garbage_threshold`, `min_blob_file_size_for_gc`, `max_blob_files_per_gc`, `blob_gc_priority`. High-garbage blob files are now collected regardless of age, prioritized by configurable criteria. The old options `blob_garbage_collection_age_cutoff` and `blob_garbage_collection_force_threshold` remain available for backwards compatibility.
